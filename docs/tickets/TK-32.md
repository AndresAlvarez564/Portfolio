## Objective
Implement the SQS email worker Lambda that reads contact form submissions from the SQS queue and sends email notifications to Andres via SES. Configure the SQS queue, DLQ, and SES sender identity in CDK.

## Proposed Scope
- Infrastructure: SQS queue, DLQ, and SES email identity in CDK `async-processing.ts`
- Backend: email worker Lambda triggered by SQS, sends notification via SES
- Observability: CloudWatch alarms for the email worker Lambda and DLQ depth

## Flow

```
Contact Lambda saves message to DynamoDB
↓
Contact Lambda sends message to SQS queue
↓
SQS triggers email worker Lambda
↓
Email worker reads message from SQS event
↓
Email worker sends notification email to Andres via SES
↓
On success: SQS deletes message from queue
↓
On SES failure: message goes to Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)
↓
DLQ alarm fires if messages accumulate
```
